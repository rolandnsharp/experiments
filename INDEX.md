## Headline results

The few results that change what we believe, each with how far it is
settled. A row is promoted to *established* only on two seeds or a full run;
*provisional* means one seed or a partial run, and the number can move. The
full record, wins and nulls alike, is the dated table below.

| result | evidence | status |
|---|---|---|
| **The complex (polar) pole beats real decay at equal compute**: 0.036–0.043 bpb at 3.4 M (steady over a 30K pass; 0.034–0.036 at 1x, 2x and 4x the peak rate); **0.103 at 100 M after 640 of 15,360 updates** (1.8794 vs 1.9829, Diakosmos's recipe). Early in training, so not yet evidence that the gain grows with size: at 3.4 M the gap was 0.064 at 1,000 updates and settled at 0.036; the full 100 M run decides. The parametrization is LRU's; the equal-compute measurement is ours | [text-pole-form](experiments/2026-10-21-text-pole-form/), [diakosmos-size-pole](experiments/2026-10-23-diakosmos-size-pole/), [learning-rate-4l32h](experiments/2026-10-23-learning-rate-4l32h/) | 3.4 M **established** (two seeds); 100 M **provisional** (640 of 15,360 updates, one seed) |
| **A twice-width ternary Vidya matches full precision at 1.7x fewer bytes, with integer projections** (it leads at 3K, level by a full pass): 1.9818 bpb at 3.9 MB vs 2.0039 at 6.77 MB (float at its best tried rate, 4x), with 96% of its multiply-adds as ternary adds; the same-width ternary model loses 0.077 | [ternary-projections](experiments/2026-10-23-ternary-projections/) | **established at 3K** (two seeds); **at 30K the lead shrinks to 0.005** (one seed); at this cache-sized model, post-training int8 of the float model is smaller (≈3.6 vs 3.9 MB) and faster at +0.0005 bpb; but on the uConsole a real ternary kernel decodes a 1.7 B model **2.4x faster than int8** ([uconsole-decode-speed](experiments/2026-10-24-uconsole-decode-speed/)), so ternary pays at sizes beyond the caches |
| **Diakosmos's learning rate (0.003) is too low at 4L/32H, and the pole gain survives tuning**: 4x gains 0.045–0.047 bpb for both pole forms; the pole gain is 0.036 / 0.036 / 0.034 / 0.037 at 1x / 2x / 4x / 8x | [learning-rate-4l32h](experiments/2026-10-23-learning-rate-4l32h/) | **provisional** (one seed, 3K updates only) |
